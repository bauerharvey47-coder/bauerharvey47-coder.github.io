Specter Social Media

Specter Social Media is a private desktop app for Windows. It helps one creator plan, produce and review videos for their own YouTube channels and TikTok accounts.

What it does

  • A team of AI assistants proposes video ideas, researches facts, writes scripts, creates voice-overs and assembles videos.
  • Nothing is published automatically. Every finished video waits for the creator's approval. On TikTok, approved videos are sent to the creator's TikTok inbox as drafts, and the creator posts them in the TikTok app.
  • With the creator's permission, it reads the creator's own channel and video statistics to learn what works, for example the best time to post.

Privacy

Specter runs entirely on the creator's own computer. There is no Specter server: no account data is sent to us, and nothing is sold or shared. Read the full Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.
