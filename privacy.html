Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

1. Who we are

Specter Social Media ("Specter") is a private desktop application for Windows, operated by Michal, Germany (contact: banepatrick3@gmail.com). Specter is used by its operator to plan, produce and review videos for the operator's own YouTube channels and TikTok accounts.

2. How Specter works

Specter runs entirely on the user's own computer. There is no Specter server. No data from your accounts is sent to us, and we do not collect, sell or share personal data.

3. Data Specter accesses

Only with your permission, and only for accounts you connect yourself:

YouTube (Google)

  • Your channel details and statistics (for example subscribers and views).
  • Statistics and YouTube Analytics reports for your videos (for example views, watch time and subscribers gained).
  • Public comments on your own videos, read only, to summarize what viewers ask for. Specter never posts or replies to comments.
  • Uploading a video you approved, only if you switch on automatic uploads.

TikTok

  • Basic profile information: display name, username and an account identifier.
  • Account statistics: follower, following, like and video counts.
  • Your public videos and their view, like, comment and share counts.
  • Uploading a video you approved to your TikTok inbox as a draft. You review and post it yourself in the TikTok app.

Specter does not access private messages, contacts, payment data or the content of other people's accounts.

4. How the data is used

Only to show you your own statistics, to learn what works for your channels (for example the best time to post), to link a published video to the production that created it, and to deliver videos you approved. The data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to train AI models.

5. Services you choose to use

Specter's AI features run on Anthropic's Claude under your own Claude account. When they plan or write content, Specter sends the context they need, for example drafts, research notes and summaries of your channel statistics, to Claude. If you add an ElevenLabs key, the script text is sent to ElevenLabs to create the voice-over. These services handle data under their own terms and privacy policies: Anthropic, ElevenLabs.

6. Google API Services and YouTube

Specter's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specter uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube channel, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can remove Specter's access at any time on your Google account permissions page.

7. Storage and security

All data stays on your own computer, in Specter's data folder. Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted with Windows data protection. Disconnecting an account in Specter deletes its tokens. Statistics are kept so charts can show history, until you delete them; deleting Specter's data folder removes everything.

8. Removing access

Google: use your Google account permissions page. TikTok: in the TikTok app, open Settings and privacy and remove Specter from the apps and services connected to your account. You can also disconnect any account inside Specter.

9. Children

Specter is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 16.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (in Germany, the authority of your federal state). Because we do not receive your account data, these rights mostly concern data on your own computer, which you control directly. You can contact us at any time at the email below.
